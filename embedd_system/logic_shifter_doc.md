Logic Level Shifter (TXS0108E) Wiring Guide for UARTThis document outlines the wiring configuration for interfacing a 5V Microcontroller (ATmega32) with a 3.3V Microcontroller (ESP8266) using the TXS0108E 8-Channel Bi-directional Logic Level Converter.1. Module OverviewThe TXS0108E module is designed to safely translate logic levels between two different voltage domains.Side A (Low Voltage): 1.2V to 3.6V (Connected to ESP8266).Side B (High Voltage): 1.65V to 5.5V (Connected to ATmega32).2. Power Connections (Crucial)Pin on ModuleConnection SourceDescriptionVAESP8266 (3V3 Pin)Power supply for the low-voltage side.VBATmega32 (5V Pin)Power supply for the high-voltage side.GNDCommon GroundConnect GND from both ESP8266 and ATmega32 here.OEESP8266 (3V3 Pin)Output Enable. Must be pulled HIGH (to 3.3V) to enable the module.3. Data Connections (UART)In UART communication, Transmit (TX) connects to Receive (RX). The Level Shifter sits in between to protect the 3.3V pins of the ESP8266 from the 5V signals of the ATmega32.Pin MappingSource PinShifter High (B)Shifter Low (A)Target PinATmega32 TX (Pin 15)B1A1ESP8266 RX (RXD)ATmega32 RX (Pin 14)B2A2ESP8266 TX (TXD)4. 5. Important NotesCommon Ground: Ensure all devices share the same Ground (GND). Data will be corrupted or "Parse Errors" will occur without a stable shared ground.Baud Rate: The current speed of 9600 bps is highly recommended for stability over jumper wires.OE Pin: Always remember to connect OE to 3.3V. If left disconnected, the module will remain in a "sleep" state and won't pass any data.
